/ What is confirmed so far

Monster Hunter Outlanders is set on Aesoland, an island where a mineral called Radiantite has changed some monsters into what the game calls Radiant species. You create your own hunter, take named Adventurers with their own abilities into a hunt, and play alone or in a squad of four. Seven weapon types are named in the official description: Great Sword, Long Sword, Dual Blades, Hammer, Lance, Heavy Bowgun and Bow. The Palico returns as a Buddy, joined by two new companions, the Rutaco and the Trillan.

The game's flagship monster is Ruger Aidal, an Elder Dragon created for Outlanders, and official footage has also shown returning monsters including Rathalos, Azure Rathalos and Great Girros. Pre-registration opened on 26 August 2026 and passed five million sign-ups within three weeks, unlocking the first reward tier. The mainland China version is scheduled for 2027 onwards.

/ How this wiki works

Every number, name and mechanic on this site comes from official information or from the game's own data. Nothing is guessed, and where something has not been published the page says so plainly. Pages show the date they were last updated, and each one is rechecked against the live game when it launches. If you spot an error, the contact page has a working address and corrections are welcome.
