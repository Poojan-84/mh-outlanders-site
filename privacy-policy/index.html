Monster Hunter Outlanders/Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Monster Hunter Outlanders Wiki ("the site", "we", "us") collects when you visit monster-hunter-outlanders.wiki, how it is used, and the choices you have. The site has no accounts, comments or sign-up forms, so we never ask for your name, email address or other contact details.

Information collected automatically

Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs standard technical information for each request (including your IP address) to keep the service secure and reliable. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: which pages are popular, roughly which country or region visitors come from, and what devices they use. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. We use these reports only in aggregate, to decide what to improve.

Fonts. The site's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address for that request.

Advertising

The site is supported by ads served by third-party advertising partners, including Google. These partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads and measure their performance.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ad Settings. You can opt out of other participating vendors' personalised ads at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.

Read more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Consent in Europe

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message before advertising cookies are used. You can accept, refuse or choose purposes there, and you can change your choice later from the privacy link the message leaves on the page.

Cookies and local storage

The cookies on this site are set by Google Analytics and our advertising partners, as described above. We don't set cookies of our own for tracking.

The site saves one preference in your browser's local storage: whether you collapsed the side menu. That value stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. Mail to our address is forwarded by our domain registrar, Namecheap, to a mailbox hosted by Google. We use it only to answer you and to act on your request, and we don't add you to any list.

How information is used and shared

Information collected through the services above is used to run and secure the site, understand its audience, and serve ads. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it with anyone beyond the service providers named in this policy.

Your rights and choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, and control ad personalisation with the links in the Advertising section.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, and to object to or limit its use. Because we don't hold names or accounts, most such information sits with the providers above. Contact us and we'll help with any request we can act on.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when our services or practices change, and the date at the top will always show the latest revision.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: contact@monster-hunter-outlanders.wiki.
