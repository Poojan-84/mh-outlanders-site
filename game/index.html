What Is Monster Hunter Outlanders?

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Monster Hunter Outlanders is a free-to-play Monster Hunter game for iOS and Android. It is developed by TiMi Studio Group, a subsidiary of Tencent Games, under licence from Capcom, and published worldwide by Level Infinite. It is officially described as Survival Hunting Action. It launches globally on 29 October 2026.

Where it sits in the series

Monster Hunter began in 2004, and the series had sold more than 131 million units by 30 June 2026. Outlanders is a licensed spin-off built for phones rather than a port of a console entry: Capcom and TiMi announced on 9 November 2022 that they were co-developing a Monster Hunter mobile game, and the Outlanders title was revealed in November 2024. It keeps the loop the series is known for. You take on a hunt, bring the monster down, and turn what it drops into better equipment for the next, harder hunt.

Aesoland and the Radiant species

The game takes place on Aesoland, an island new to the series. A mineral called Radiantite is found there, and monsters altered by it are known as Radiant species. Official footage has shown Radiant variants alongside ordinary monsters, and the game's flagship, the Elder Dragon Ruger Aidal, carries golden crystals across its body that it wields like swords. One named area has been shown so far, the Abyss of Decay.

Adventurers

You create your own protagonist, and alongside that hunter Outlanders has Adventurers: named characters, each with individual abilities and a weapon specialty, chosen before a hunt. Three have been named in official material so far, and the protagonist was shown fighting Azure Rathalos with a Hammer. Each weapon is officially said to form its own combination with the Adventurers, opening up new follow-up moves.

The companions that travel with you are described officially as gathered from different regions.

Buddies

The official game description names three Buddies. The Palico, the cat-like partner that has accompanied hunters since the early games, returns. Two are new to Outlanders: the Rutaco and the Trillan. Footage of Freya's Rathalos hunt shows a Buddy native to Aesoland fighting at her side.

Exploring and building

Outlanders adds exploration and crafting on top of hunting. Two exploration features have been shown by name: the Gliding Challenge, and a craftable structure called the Wind Platform. Controls are designed for touch screens. You can roam the open field alone, or form a hunting squad of four players, which the official description says takes a single tap.

Closed betas before launch

The game went through several closed beta tests. Registration for the second opened in April 2026. The third took sign-ups from 2 to 24 July 2026 and began on 7 August 2026 in Japan, the United States, Canada, Germany, the United Kingdom, France and the Philippines. Details shown in a beta can change before release, so this wiki treats beta information as provisional until it is checked against the launch version. The dated steps from announcement to release are on the release date page.
