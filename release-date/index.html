Monster Hunter Outlanders/Release date

Monster Hunter Outlanders Release Date

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Monster Hunter Outlanders launches globally on 29 October 2026 for iOS and Android. The date was announced on 29 September 2026, thirty days before launch. The game is free-to-play and contains in-app purchases.

Launch details

Global launch29 October 2026
PlatformsiOS (App Store), Android (Google Play)
PriceFree-to-play, with in-app purchases
Same-day regionsJapan, the United States and Europe
Southeast AsiaPublished by Garena, with its own pre-registration
Mainland ChinaScheduled for 2027 onwards

Platforms and price

Outlanders is a mobile game. It has been announced for iOS and Android only, and its controls are designed for touch screens. It costs nothing to download and play, and it is supported by in-app purchases.

Regions

The 29 October date applies to Japan, the United States and Europe on the same day. In Southeast Asia the game is published by Garena, which opened its own pre-registration for the region. Mainland China is the exception: that version is scheduled separately, for 2027 onwards.

The road to launch

From the first announcement of the partnership to launch day is just under four years. Pre-registration runs for 64 days, from 26 August to 29 October 2026.

DateWhat happened
9 November 2022Capcom and TiMi Studio Group announce that they are co-developing a Monster Hunter game for mobile. No title is given.
November 2024The game is revealed as Monster Hunter Outlanders.
April 2026Registration opens for the second closed beta test.
2 to 24 July 2026Registration period for the third closed beta test.
7 August 2026The third closed beta begins in Japan, the United States, Canada, Germany, the United Kingdom, France and the Philippines.
26 August 2026Pre-registration opens on the App Store and Google Play.
17 September 2026Five million pre-registrations are announced, the Elder Dragon Ruger Aidal is shown in motion for the first time, and a playable demo opens at Tokyo Game Show (17 to 21 September).
29 September 2026The global launch date is announced.
29 October 2026Global launch on iOS and Android.
2027 onwardsScheduled release of the mainland China version.

Getting ready for launch day

Pre-registration is open on the App Store and Google Play. Signing up on the store you use puts the game in your library ahead of release, and pre-registration totals unlock reward tiers for players; the tiers and their contents are listed on the pre-registration rewards page.

If you are new to the series or want to know what kind of game to expect, the game overview covers the setting, Adventurers and Buddies.
