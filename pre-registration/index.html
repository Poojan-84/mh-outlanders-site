Monster Hunter Outlanders/Pre-registration rewards

Pre-Registration Rewards

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Monster Hunter Outlanders opened pre-registration on 26 August 2026 on the App Store and Google Play. Rewards are tied to the total number of sign-ups: each milestone the community reaches unlocks another tier. Four tiers have been announced, from 5 million to 20 million.

Milestones and rewards

Pre-registrationsRewardStatus
5 millionStandard Contract ×5, 5,000 ZennyReached (announced 17 September 2026)
10 millionAdventurer RayaAnnounced tier

20 millionStandard Contract ×5, Aqua Dew ×20Announced tier

The first tier fell quickly. Five million sign-ups were announced on 17 September 2026, twenty-two days after pre-registration opened.

What you get if every tier is reached

Three of the four tiers include five Standard Contracts, so the full set adds up to more than any single line suggests:

  • 15 Standard Contracts (five each from the 5, 15 and 20 million tiers)
  • 5,000 Zenny (5 million tier)
  • 20 Growth Concentrate (15 million tier)
  • 20 Aqua Dew (20 million tier)
  • The Adventurer Raya (10 million tier)

What the rewards are

Raya is a playable Adventurer, one of the named characters you hunt as in Outlanders. A full character is the most valuable single item on the list, which is why it sits alone on its own tier.

Zenny is the money of the Monster Hunter series, the currency hunters have been paid in since the first game.

Standard Contract, Growth Concentrate and Aqua Dew are Outlanders items. Their names are confirmed by the reward list; what each one does in the game has not been set out in official material, so this page does not guess at it.

How to pre-register

Pre-register on the store for your device: the App Store on iPhone and iPad, or Google Play on Android. Pre-registration is free. In Southeast Asia the game is published by Garena, which runs its own pre-registration for that region.

The game launches on 29 October 2026. Dates, regions and the mainland China schedule are on the release date page, and the game overview explains what Adventurers are and how they fit into a hunt.
